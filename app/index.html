How to use this train condition monitoring app

This web application enables railway fleet managers and engineers to deploy trained condition monitoring models across rail vehicle subsystems.

1. Subsystems Covered

  • Door: Ingests continuous telemetry CSV streams, segments open/close cycles (>1000ms delta), and flags abnormal mechanical resistance cycles using logistic regression.
  • ACV: Evaluates multi-car cooling telemetry (.xlsx) to isolate and localize which specific car is suffering from a refrigerant leak.
  • SHM: Computes cumulative fatigue damage estimation on dynamic stress CSV series via ASTM E1049 Rainflow cycle counting and material S-N power law.
  • Rail Corrugation: Track condition classification (model not implemented yet).

2. How to generate a prediction

  1. Select the desired active subsystem (Door, ACV, or SHM).
  2. Upload your test file(s) via the dropzone.
  3. Click "Run Prediction".
  4. View the diagnostic verdict banner and official prediction table.
  5. Click "Download Prediction (CSV)" to export the results.
