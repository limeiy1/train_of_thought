How to use this train condition monitoring app

This web application enables railway fleet managers, depot engineers, and maintenance operators to deploy trained machine learning models across four independent rail vehicle subsystems with ease.

1. Subsystems Covered

  • Door: Detects open/close cycles in a continuous telemetry stream and flags mechanical resistance anomalies (such as debris obstruction or actuator friction).
  • ACV: Evaluates multi-car cooling telemetry to isolate and localize which specific car is suffering from a refrigerant leak.
  • Rail Corrugation: Classifies track condition into Normal, Side I Corrugation (Left), or Side II Corrugation (Right) from axle-box vibration + shock.
  • SHM: Uses cumulative fatigue damage regression on dynamic stress time series.

2. How to generate a prediction

  1. Click any of the 4 subsystem cards at the top.
  2. Either drag and drop a .csv file or click on the box to upload from your computer.
  3. Click "Run Prediction".
  4. View the results, and download the predictions as a .CSV.
  5. Click on "Test Another File" to restart.
